# Bible data sources

The static Bible database in `data/` is generated from verse-per-line source files published by [eBible.org](https://ebible.org/).

## Chinese

- Title: 新标点和合本 / Chinese Union Version (Simplified)
- eBible ID: `cmn-cu89s`
- License: Public Domain
- Source: https://ebible.org/bible/details.php?id=cmn-cu89s

## English

- Title: World English Bible
- eBible ID: `engwebp`
- License: Public Domain
- Source: https://ebible.org/bible/details.php?id=engwebp

The two translations have a small number of traditional versification differences. Records are merged by canonical book, chapter, and verse identifiers without inventing or rewriting missing text. `data/manifest.json` records the generated database statistics.
